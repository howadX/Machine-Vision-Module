## Method

The evaluator consumes frozen replay fixtures only. It validates manifest
identity, fixture status, monotonic frame/event sequences, source stamps (no
future source), and explicit event-gap reporting. Malformed input fails closed.
Mutable `current_state.md`, `current_cortex.json`, and `labels.jsonl` are not
benchmark inputs.

The six phase-gate claims are explicitly pinned as uncertainty honesty, forecast
IoU, attention recall@3, affordance accuracy, memory precision@5 and advisory
re-observation. Temporal QA, identity continuity, object permanence and
self-model remain separately reported replay metrics. All rows are synthetic
replay, never live-camera generalisation claims.
