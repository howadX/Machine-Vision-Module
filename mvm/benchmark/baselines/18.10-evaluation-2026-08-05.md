## Method

The evaluator consumes frozen replay fixtures only. It validates manifest
identity, fixture status, monotonic frame/event sequences, source stamps (no
future source), and explicit event-gap reporting. Malformed input fails closed.
Mutable `current_state.md`, `current_cortex.json`, and `labels.jsonl` are not
benchmark inputs.

The six core claims are explicitly pinned as temporal QA, identity continuity,
object permanence, uncertainty honesty, forecast IoU, and attention recall@3.
Skeleton scenarios are excluded from denominators rather than counted as
failures or successes.

## Runtime gate

The same-hardware 18.8b.1 controlled run remains the runtime evidence: the
largest adaptive-vs-fixed wall-time delta was `+0.31%` over 300 camera frames,
within the `≤5%` requirement, with no agent errors. This is not conflated with
replay accuracy.

## Gate verdict

The evaluator and replay safety gate are implemented and green, but Phase 18 is
**not yet claim-closed**. The required `4 of 6 core claims` gate cannot be
honestly evaluated while three core scenario families have no frames. No live
camera assistance was required for this checkpoint; completing the gate needs
either deterministic captured replay fixtures or an explicitly approved
synthetic corpus for scenarios 001/004/007/009/011/012.
