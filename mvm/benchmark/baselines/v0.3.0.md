## Runtime reference

The last live-validated project documentation reports approximately 16–17 FPS
for the human-processing mesh and approximately 25 FPS frame delivery on the
development machine. This is historical evidence, not a fresh controlled run.

Before merging the 18.1 runtime migration, rerun the same camera/config/model
profile and record median and p95 over at least five minutes. The acceptance
threshold is no more than 5% degradation against that same-run baseline. CI
stub timing is tracked separately and must not be presented as camera throughput.

## Baseline interpretation

The v0.3.0 result is intentionally incomplete. Phase 18 earns claims by filling
the scenario fixtures and evaluator outputs; it does not backfill convenient
numbers into this document.

The four `Synthetic` rows are executable contract baselines aggregated by
`canonical_scene_contract_v0_3_0_synthetic_baseline_is_executable`. They encode
known v0.3.0 capabilities and absences, not camera measurements, and therefore
must not be compared with later live-corpus results without the `synthetic`
qualifier.
